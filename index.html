    How to play

    Goal

    Two teams of five. Minion waves march down three lanes every 25 seconds. Break the two towers in any lane, then destroy the enemy core to win.

    Roles

    Controls

    Move
    WASD or right-click the ground. On touch, use the joystick on the left.
    Attack
    Space (hold) or click an enemy. Heroes are targeted first.
    Skills
    Q E and R (ultimate, level 4). Hold to aim at the cursor, release to cast. On touch, drag a skill button to aim, or tap it to auto-aim.
    Recall
    B teleports you to base after 3.5 seconds.
    Regen
    H restores 35% of your health over 5 seconds, even while you walk. Attacking, casting or taking damage from heroes and towers stops it. Every hero has it (25s cooldown).
    Battle spell
    F casts the special ability you picked before the match: Blink, Smite, Reap, Cleanse, Sprint, Aegis or Stonegaze.
    Scoreboard
    Tab

    Tips

    • Landing the final hit on a minion gives you its gold. Press P to shop, or confirm the suggested item in the top-right corner.
    • Towers hit minions first, but switch to you if you attack an enemy hero nearby.
    • Junglers: the Azure Wisp speeds up your skills, the Ember Brute makes your attacks burn.
    • The Tidewarden (2:00) pays the whole team. The Lord (8:00) makes your whole team hit harder.
